How EventSphere Works

1. Create Event

The organizer creates an event by entering the event name, type, date, expected participants and budget.

2. Select Venue

EventSphere checks venue capacity, availability and date conflicts before assigning a venue.

3. Allocate Resources

Resources are allocated according to their available quantities.

4. Manage Participants

Participants can register and receive tickets with QR codes for event check-in.

5. Manage Vendors

Organizers can assign vendors and evaluate their performance.

6. Monitor Finance

Track budgets, expenses and revenues associated with each event.
